No words, signatures, or watermarks. Style: Cult of the Lamb-inspired cute 2D game art, with thick, bold dark-brown outlines, chunky rounded shapes, flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone, and a saturated, cute palette, for a cozy living room at night.
